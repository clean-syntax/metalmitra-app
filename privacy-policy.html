Privacy Policy for MetalMitra

Effective Date: November 16, 2026

Welcome to MetalMitra. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection and usage for the MetalMitra mobile application.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

MetalMitra is designed as an offline utility tool for gold and silver rate calculation and estimate generation. We do not require account creation, logins, or authentication. We do not collect, harvest, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, phone number, contacts, or location.

2. Local Storage and Data Ownership

All data entered into the application—including metal rates, purity values, making charges, custom shop information (Shop Name, GSTIN, Phone), customer names, and past calculation history—is stored exclusively on your device's local memory (SQLite/Room storage). We have no access to this data, and it is never uploaded to any remote server or third-party cloud service.

3. Device Permissions & Receipt Sharing

MetalMitra may require the following basic permissions solely for core features:

  • Storage / Media Access: Strictly used to save generated digital estimate slips (receipt images) to your device storage and enable sharing via installed messaging applications (such as WhatsApp) upon user prompt.

4. Advertising and Analytics

MetalMitra contains no third-party advertisements, tracking pixels, or user behavior analytics SDKs. Your app usage patterns remain completely private.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application does not address anyone under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
